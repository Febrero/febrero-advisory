Privacy notice

Febrero AI · Pedro Febrero · last updated 28 September 2026

This notice explains how I use business contact details when I write to companies about AI implementation. It applies to the emails I send to businesses in the United Kingdom and elsewhere.

Who I am

Pedro Febrero, trading as Febrero AI, based in Lisbon, Portugal. I decide how this data is used (I am the data controller). Contact: pedro@febreroai.com.

What data I use and where it comes from

  • Company details (name, registration number, registered address, sector) and the names and roles of company directors, from the public Companies House register.
  • The company's business email address, phone number and website, as published on the company's own website or public business listings (for example Google Maps).

I only contact businesses at their business addresses. I do not buy personal data and I do not use sensitive data.

Why I use it, and the legal basis

To introduce my services to businesses that may benefit from them and to follow up once, a few days later. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in offering business services to other businesses (UK GDPR and EU GDPR, article 6(1)(f)). I send at most two emails unless you reply.

Who else handles it

I do not sell or share your data. Service providers process it on my behalf: email delivery (Resend, EU region), email forwarding (ImprovMX) and website hosting (GitHub Pages). The data is processed in the European Union.

How long I keep it

For as long as we are in contact, and no more than 24 months after my last email. If you ask me to stop, I keep only your email address on a suppression list, so that you are never contacted again.

Your rights

You can ask me to stop at any time: reply "unsubscribe" to any email or use the unsubscribe link, and you will not hear from me again. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the data I hold about you by writing to pedro@febreroai.com. If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK or the CNPD (cnpd.pt) in Portugal.

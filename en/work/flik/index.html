FLIK: short AI videos

FLIK (flik.social) is the AI video studio I built: from a business's photos or links, it makes short vertical videos (15 to 25 seconds) for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, in 17 styles — from a virtual presenter to a movie trailer, a viral dance or a soap opera. Below, one example of each, made with a real business's photos.

The FLIK video studioflik.social

Send the business's photos or links, pick a style and get a vertical video ready to post, in English, Portuguese or Spanish. The presenter styles are open now; the others open in phases, partners first.
AI soap operaflik.social · TikTok · Reels

The products become characters in a soap-opera episode that ends on «to be continued…», each talking with its own face. Example: Santini, in Portuguese.

Viral danceflik.social · TikTok · Reels

A mascot made from the product does this week's viral dance in front of the shop, on the beat. Example: Manteigaria, in Portuguese.

Liminal Dreamflik.social · Reels · TikTok

The place's empty rooms reimagined as a dream: still water, fog, endless corridors, a sky underfoot. No voice. Example: DEZ Studio, in Portuguese.

Retro 1950sflik.social · Reels · TikTok

«The place of tomorrow»: a 1950s poster of the city, the rooms as 1957 Kodachrome and a real photo as the postcard. No voice. Example: DEZ Studio, in Portuguese.

Mixedflik.social · Reels · Facebook

Starts with the presenter full screen and fast transitions, and ends with her in a bubble over the photos. Example: Alimor Café, in English.

Movie trailerflik.social · Shorts · Reels

Black title cards, slow motion, a deep narrator and a playful rating card. Example: Action Black, in Spanish.

90s animeflik.social · TikTok · Shorts

The business as an anime opening: an episode title, speed lines and one photo turning into its anime version. Example: Manga Ramen, in Portuguese.

Top 5flik.social · TikTok · Reels

The presenter opens, then the place's best five are counted down from #5 to #1, with a drumroll before the winner. Example: Dino Parque Lourinhã, in Portuguese.

Before & afterflik.social · Reels · Facebook

Each before photo wipes into its after on the beat; with archive photos, it tells the place's story. Example: Lisbon Zoo, in Portuguese.

Photo dumpflik.social · Reels · TikTok

The photos drop as 3D Polaroids, with a camera click on every beat. Example: Portugal dos Pequenitos, in Portuguese.

Memesflik.social · TikTok · Reels

Meme formats everyone knows («Nobody:», expectation vs reality, POV) with the business's own photos. Example: O Rei das Bifanas, in Portuguese.

What does $X get you?flik.social · TikTok · Reels

A budget, real prices and a running total: what fits in it. Example: Animal Cocina, in Spanish.

Beatflik.social · Reels · TikTok

The photos cut to the beat of a track, the presenter's opening line and words that hit with the drop. Example: Dugo Motos, in Spanish.

Vaporwaveflik.social · Reels · TikTok

An 80s–90s look: a neon grid, a striped sun and the photos opening as retro computer windows, with no voice. Example: RetroCity Lisboa.

Meme-style late-night weirdness: a VHS look, a mascot and one word per beat. Example: Bolera Mayorca, in Spanish.
